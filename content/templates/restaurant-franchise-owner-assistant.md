## What it does

For an owner running several restaurant locations. Each morning it rounds up messages waiting on a call - bank notices, supplier updates, payroll items - into one digest. Monthly it works through delivery-platform sales tax per store and turns point-of-sale figures into side-by-side reports. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
