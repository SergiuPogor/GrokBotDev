## What it does

Sits quietly against the official Fleet API and checks on your car without ever waking it, so range and climate stay where you set them. Normal days are silent; a message arrives only when something genuinely deserves a human, like an unexpected change of state. Read-only by design, it adjusts nothing. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
