## What it does

A standing watch on the accounts that actually break AI news: labs, tool makers and a few notable researchers, about 270 of them. It pulls out launches, coding-agent and local-model updates and benchmark results, passing on only what is worth your time. Polling stays frugal, near a quarter of the daily X allowance. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @gota_bara shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
