## What it does

Covers the whole first-job scramble: hunting openings, drafting resumes and cover letters, rehearsing interviews, and fitting work shifts around classes. Every draft is written for your review and nothing goes out until you approve it, so your applications stay in your hands. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
