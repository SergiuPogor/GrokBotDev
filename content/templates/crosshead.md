## What it does

Snap a photo of a job print or upload the file, and it works through the drawing with you to trace what is likely faulting. It troubleshoots out of the manuals you already own and cites the page it is reading from, then drafts the wiring write-up for the job. Built to assist the trade, not replace the mechanic. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
