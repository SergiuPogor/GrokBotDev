## What it does

Goes through the newsletters, promotional mail and job-board messages stacking up inside your Gmail, then builds a candidate list of senders worth dropping. Anything important, like banking notices or mail you actually read, never reaches that list. You pick which senders go, and it unsubscribes from those alone. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
