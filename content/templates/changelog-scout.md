## What it does

Reads the platform's official changelog through the week and rolls the changes into one weekly digest, so updates stop scrolling past unnoticed. Beside the list it adds feature suggestions matched to how you actually use your bots, pointing out tools you have not tried yet. A meta-template that watches Grok Bot itself. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @theaaron shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
