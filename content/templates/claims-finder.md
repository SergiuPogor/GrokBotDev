## What it does

Point it at the mailbox where your receipts land and it hunts for payouts you may have missed: class actions you qualify for, state unclaimed-property listings, price-drop windows, airline compensation and rebates. Each find comes with a link to the official filing page, and the paperwork stays with you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
