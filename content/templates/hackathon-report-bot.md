## What it does

Point it at an event hashtag and it watches new posts while the hackathon runs, condensing each into a one-line summary on a growing thread, with a link back to the original post. When the end time you set arrives it stops posting by itself. Built for organizers and teams who want the day recounted automatically. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
