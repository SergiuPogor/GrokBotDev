## What it does

Tracks the dates a new owner tends to lose: maintenance intervals, registration renewals, inspections and insurance, all visible in one list. When a shop hands over a quote, it explains the line items in plain words so you can tell necessary work from optional. It also covers moving a car to a different state. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
