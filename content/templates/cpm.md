## What it does

Give it a creator profile on Instagram or TikTok and it works out what their views are actually worth, then prices the partnership from that. You get a suggested fee, a three-month package list with paid-boost options broken out, and an opening message you can send as-is. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
