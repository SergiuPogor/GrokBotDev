## What it does

Give it nearly finished writing and it pulls out each checkable statement, then labels it: backed up, stretched past what the sources say, plainly wrong, or unverifiable from what it can reach. Nothing to install or connect - no plug-ins, no keys - so a last pass can run in the seconds before you hit publish. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @GrokBotRadar shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
