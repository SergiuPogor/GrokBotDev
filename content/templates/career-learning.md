## What it does

Choose the move you want - a bigger title, a jump to another field, or more depth in the seat you already hold - and it works out which abilities stand between you and it. Then it stays on the case: regular check-ins on what you have practised, what comes next, and a nudge when you let the plan drift. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
