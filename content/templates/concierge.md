## What it does

Tell it the city and what you fancy - a table for dinner, an activity, somewhere locals actually go - and it returns a tight shortlist with ratings and ballpark costs attached. Settle on one and it writes the reservation message, leaving the sending to you. It never spends or books anything without your go-ahead. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @TedSkeen shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
