## What it does

Before an automated release goes out the door, this gate demands receipts - a green pipeline, a verification run, how the branch actually merged, and a written account of what shipped. Anything missing and the release lane is held until the evidence exists or a human overrules the block. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
