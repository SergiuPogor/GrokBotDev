## What it does

Links an assistant into Feishu, the chat app also called Lark, so it sits in your contacts like a colleague. Send it a private message to research something, draft a document, or kick off a job; the reply lands in that same thread. Setup walks you through registering your own app in Feishu's developer console. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
