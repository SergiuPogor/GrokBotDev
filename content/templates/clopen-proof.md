## What it does

Built around the shift pattern that wrecks rest: closing late, then opening early the next morning. Enter your rotations and it places them on the calendar, rings off protected sleep and meal time around the worst turnarounds, and guards a wind-down block before bed. A night that looks too short earns you a warning. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
