## What it does

Paid coding plans sometimes get usage counters reset for everyone at once, announced only in a post on X. It watches the vendors' official accounts and flags the moment a reset lands, so you can aim heavy jobs at the fresh allowance. Covers the well-known plans from Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Cursor and Google. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @bylixing shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
