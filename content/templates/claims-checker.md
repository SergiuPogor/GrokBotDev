## What it does

A gate your drafts pass through before anything goes out. Hand it a draft, a headline, a pitch — anything bound for the public — and it works claim by claim, ruling each one backed, unbacked or outright false. It remembers what you already substantiated, so a proven fact needs no second round. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
