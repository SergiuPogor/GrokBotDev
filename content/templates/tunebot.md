## What it does

A self-reflection conversation, not an advice column. You bring a pattern you keep repeating - say, the sting of a peer winning - and it asks questions until the reflex itself is on the table and named. It closes with one small experiment for the next time the trigger shows up. The frame comes from Tuning Theory. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
