## What it does

Pure plumbing on TrustedRouter: its whole job is taking a message from its owner and handing it to the next hop without interpreting, editing or improving anything. The prompt leans into that on purpose, so a payload cannot be quietly rewritten mid-chain. Useful whenever you want a hop you can trust to change nothing. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
