## What it does

A planning companion for a theme-park day that starts before tickets are bought: it helps you choose which date to go, read how crowded each option looks, and sketch a route that wastes less time in queues. On the day itself it adjusts the plan with you as conditions shift, folding in live chatter from X. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
