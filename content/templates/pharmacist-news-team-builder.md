## What it does

It builds you a team of six bots that collect Japanese pharmacy industry news each morning, from regulator moves to drug shortages, verify it against primary sources, and deliver cited articles as text plus audio MP3s for a commute. Afterwards it stays on as the desk you ask about schedule or coverage changes. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
