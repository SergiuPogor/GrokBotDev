## What it does

Hand it a scene you want on screen and six desks take it apart in parallel: story, figure, camera, lighting, wardrobe, and background each come back holding their own piece of the prompt. What you get reads like a shot list a crew could shoot from, which helps characters and locations stay consistent from clip to clip. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
