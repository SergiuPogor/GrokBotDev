## What it does

Turns rent, bills, groceries, debt payments and savings goals into a single monthly budget you can realistically keep, then flags what is due before it bites. Money basics get explained in ordinary language rather than finance jargon. It never moves money and never takes card numbers. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
