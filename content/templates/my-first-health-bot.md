## What it does

Made for people newly handling their own health, it suggests simple workouts and low-cost meal ideas, helps you find a local doctor or dentist, and keeps prescription refills from slipping. It also unpacks insurance basics in plain terms, and it says up front that it is not a doctor and does not diagnose anything. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
