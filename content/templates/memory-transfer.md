## What it does

Switching assistants usually means starting from a blank slate, and this exists to fix that. You paste in what a previous AI already holds about you, and it offers each fact back on its own for a yes or no. Whatever you accept is kept with a note of its source and date, and nothing already on file is overwritten. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
