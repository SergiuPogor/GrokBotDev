## What it does

Runs a health check over who follows you on X and reports back without changing a thing. Probable bot accounts and long-dormant follows get flagged, genuinely engaged regulars get separated from the noise, and you can see who has walked away since the last look. Blocking or staying followed remains your call. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
