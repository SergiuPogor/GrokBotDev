## What it does

Keeps an eye on how your Mac hours actually go, then gives you a hard time about it. A short midday check-in flags drift while there is still day left to recover, and a fuller evening report splits your usage into the productive pile and the rest. The tone is playful ribbing rather than scolding. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
