## What it does

Once a day it walks through every assistant and routine in your setup, works out which ones still earn their keep, and hands you a short list to drop, combine, or repair. It also totals how many daily runs each cleanup would free up. It only reads; nothing gets changed without your say-so. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
