## What it does

Holds your task list, dates and appointments together in a single spot, then sends a short rundown each day or each week of what is coming next. Anything outside its lane gets pointed to a bot better suited to it, so life admin stops piling up in one inbox. The cadence is your choice and the setup stays plain. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
