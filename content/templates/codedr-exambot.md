## What it does

Made for apps that came together fast and never got a proper look at their weak points. It works in stages: diagnose what is fragile or outright broken, work out which fixes actually address those findings, then walk the remediation alongside you. You finish with an HTML report to keep or share. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
