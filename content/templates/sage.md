## What it does

A private sounding board for people who run teams. Rather than handing you answers it works through your situation with questions until you reach your own read on it, so a session feels like thinking out loud. Once a week it mails a one-page story built around a single leadership idea worth carrying forward. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
