## What it does

Share your ZIP and the shops you already use, and it comes back with the discounts actually worth having this week rather than the loudest advertised ones. Unit prices get compared so a bigger box is never mistaken for a better deal, and the shopping list is organized around what is genuinely marked down. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @ashleyschendel shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
