## What it does

A standing watch on live events in your area. Name the acts you follow and it keeps an eye on their touring, surfaces concerts and comedy shows close to you, and gives you a heads-up before tickets go on sale - so you are buying in the first minutes, not after the good seats are gone. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
