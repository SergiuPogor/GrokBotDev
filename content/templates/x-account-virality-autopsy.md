## What it does

Give it any handle and it digs through that account's history to date the moment it took off and single out the posts that did the lifting. It then lines your own profile up against the same pattern and returns a handful of concrete changes to try. Expect a blunt read; the tone leans snarky rather than gentle. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @mr_easonyang shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
