## What it does

Point it at the documents and URLs that describe your service and it fields customer questions on X Chat in writing at any hour. If a customer would rather talk it through, it moves the reply onto a voice call. It amounts to a standing support desk for an operator who cannot watch the inbox all night. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
