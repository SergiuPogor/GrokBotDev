## What it does

Works as the single front desk for a team of bots built around MBTI personalities. You bring it a request and it relays the work to whichever typed persona fits, standing in for you so you never juggle the team directly. It surfaced through a Tokyo hackathon circle as the hub of that bot set. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
